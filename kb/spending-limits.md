# Spending limits

Limits on this demo are fictional.

Lumen Reserve is a credit card. Jordan Hale's demo credit limit is $5,000. The verified chat can read the limit on the profile. Purchases that would put the balance over the limit are declined.

Lumen Everyday is a debit card spending a stored balance. The ATM daily limit is $500. The purchase daily limit is $2,500. Outbound transfers are capped at $3,000 a day.

New accounts keep the lower introductory limits for the first 30 days: ATM $200 and purchases $1,000. A freeze does not change the limit. A replacement card keeps the same limit.

Asking the assistant to raise a limit in chat is not supported. The desk can explain the published caps only.
