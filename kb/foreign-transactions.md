# Foreign transactions

Lumen Reserve charges a 1.5% foreign transaction fee on purchases made outside the United States or in a currency other than US dollars. The fee is calculated on the US dollar amount after the network conversion. A $100.00 foreign purchase posts as $101.50, which is the purchase plus the 1.5% fee.

Lumen Everyday charges a 1.0% foreign transaction fee on foreign purchases and foreign ATM withdrawals. An Everyday ATM withdrawal can also include the $2.50 Lumen out-of-network ATM fee and the operator fee.

The fee is not a tip and it is not optional. Dynamic currency conversion, where a foreign terminal offers to charge you in US dollars, can add a separate merchant markup. Lumen's fee can still apply. Choosing local currency at the terminal is usually clearer, but that is a description of the fee, not a personal recommendation.

Foreign transactions can be disputed the same way as domestic ones when the charge itself is wrong. The FX fee line follows the underlying purchase if that purchase is reversed.
