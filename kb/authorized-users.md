# Authorized users

An authorized user can carry a Reserve card on the same account. They can spend up to the account credit limit. They are not a joint owner and are not liable for the balance in this fictional policy.

Adding someone requires the primary customer to be verified. This chat cannot add an authorized user. A specialist can take the request. The authorized card has its own last four and can be frozen without freezing the primary card.

Authorized-user purchases earn rewards on the primary account.
