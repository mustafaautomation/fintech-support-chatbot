# Merchant credits

A merchant refund posts as a credit, usually within 5 business days after the merchant sends it. The credit goes to the card that paid. It does not always restore rewards points that were already redeemed.

If a refund was promised and has not posted after 10 business days, you can open a dispute with the reason that the refund is missing. An autopay payment is not a merchant credit.

Credits do not speed up a replacement card.
