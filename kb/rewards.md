# Rewards

Lumen Rewards are fictional points. You earn 1 point per dollar on purchases and 3 points on groceries and transit. Payments, cash advances, fees, and disputed amounts that are later removed do not earn points.

Redemption math: 100 points equal $1 of statement credit. Points are not cash, not a bank deposit, and not an investment. The assistant can tell you the rate and your demo balance. It will not tell you whether to redeem, hold, or invest the value.

Statement credit typically posts within two business days of a redemption request made with a specialist. This chat can explain the rate. It does not move points by itself.

Points that are still unredeemed when an account closes are forfeited. Redeem before you ask to close the account if you want the credit.
