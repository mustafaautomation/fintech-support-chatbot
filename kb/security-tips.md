# Security tips

Lumen never asks for your full card number, your password, or a one-time code by email. In this fictional sandbox the demo code is printed on the page so reviewers can try the flow. A real issuer would not publish a live code.

If a text or call says your card is locked and asks you to read the card number, hang up and freeze the card here instead. Check that you are on the Lumen help chat you opened yourself.

Use the freeze when a charge looks unfamiliar and you still have the card. Use the lost-card report when the plastic or the phone wallet is gone. Turn on transaction alerts in the fictional app. Do not share screenshots that show a full PAN. The assistant masks those numbers if they appear.

Phishing pages often misspell the brand or push a countdown. Lumen support will not threaten to close the account in the next ten minutes.
