# Statements

Lumen Reserve statements close on the same calendar day each month. The demo profile closes on the 18th. The statement shows purchases, payments, fees, and interest for that cycle.

You can ask a specialist for a copy of a closed statement. This chat does not email attachments. A payment that posts after the statement closes appears on the next one.

Review the statement within 60 days if you think a line is a billing error. That window is the fictional policy modeled on the billing-error article, not legal advice.
