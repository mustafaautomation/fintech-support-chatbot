# Payments and autopay

Autopay on Lumen Reserve can be the minimum due, the full statement balance, or a fixed amount. Jordan Hale's demo profile pays the statement balance from Everyday ···8821 on the 20th.

The cutoff is 11:59 p.m. Pacific time on the due date. A payment started after the cutoff can post the next business day and can count as late. The late fee is up to $30.

Autopay does not cover a new purchase that posts after the statement closed. It does not stop interest on a cash advance. Turning autopay off in chat is not available. A specialist can change the setting.

An autopay payment itself is not something the assistant can dispute as a purchase. If the payment went to the wrong account or was duplicated, say so and the desk will review it as a payment error.
