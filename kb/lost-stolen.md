# Lost or stolen card replacement

If your card is lost or stolen, tell the assistant "my card was stolen" or "I lost my card" after the identity check. Lumen freezes Reserve immediately and orders a replacement.

The replacement arrives in 5 business days in this demo, and the replacement fee is $0. The order id looks like REP-22041. The old card ending 4419 cannot be unfrozen. Remove it from Apple Pay, Google Pay, and any saved merchant wallets.

A stolen card with a single unrecognized purchase can also be disputed as its own case. Identity theft, an account takeover, or a request for the fraud department goes to a specialist instead of staying with the assistant.

Lumen will never ask you to read the full card number aloud or paste it into chat.
