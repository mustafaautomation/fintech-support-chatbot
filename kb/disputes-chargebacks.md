# Disputes and chargebacks

This article is a plain-language sketch of Lumen's fictional policy. It is written in the style of US consumer rules so the demo can explain timelines. It is not legal advice, not a law-firm summary, and not a promise about your case.

## Regulation Z style billing errors on Lumen Reserve

Lumen Reserve is the credit card. A billing-error notice should be sent within 60 days of the statement that first showed the error. Lumen acknowledges the notice in writing. The fictional target is 1 business day, and the policy modeled on Regulation Z allows up to 30 days.

Lumen aims to resolve the case within two billing cycles and no later than 90 days. While the amount is in dispute you do not have to pay that amount or the finance charge on it, and Lumen does not report that amount as delinquent. The assistant opens these cases with an id like LZ-48001.

A chargeback is the card-network process that can run alongside the billing-error review. Opening a case in this chat starts Lumen's review. It does not mean the merchant has already lost.

## Regulation E style errors and provisional credit on Lumen Everyday

Lumen Everyday is the debit card, so unauthorized electronic transfers follow a Regulation E-style timeline. Report the error within 60 days of the statement. Lumen investigates, generally within 10 business days. If the review needs longer, Lumen provisionally credits the amount while the investigation continues, up to 45 days (longer only for a new account or a foreign POS transfer). Everyday case ids look like LE-48002.

## What the assistant can file

Unrecognized or unauthorized charges, duplicates, cancelled orders, wrong amounts, and goods not received. Payments such as autopay are not billing errors. The assistant will not predict whether you will win and will not tell you to sue.
