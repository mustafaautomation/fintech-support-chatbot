# Account closure

To close Lumen, pay the Reserve balance to $0, including any pending purchases that later post. Unredeemed points are forfeited at closure, so redeem them first if you want statement credit. Download statements before the account closes. The demo does not have a download button. A specialist can note the request.

Closure takes about 7 days after the balance is zero. There is no monthly fee, so there is no fee to close. An open dispute can finish after the cards are closed. Do not close the account if you still want the card to pay a merchant refund back onto it. Refunds on a closed card are sent another way and take longer.

The assistant cannot close the account in chat. Asking to close, or asking about a deceased customer, a court order, or a levy, goes to a specialist.
