# Card fees

Lumen Card is a fictional neobank. These fees are synthetic help-center copy for the demo.

The annual fee on Lumen Reserve and Lumen Everyday is $0. There is no monthly maintenance fee.

The late fee is up to $30, and it is not charged when the payment arrives by the due date shown on the statement. A returned payment fee is $25.

A cash advance on Lumen Reserve costs 5% of the advance, with a $10 minimum. The cash-advance APR is higher than the purchase APR and starts accruing immediately. Describing that fee is not a suggestion to take a cash advance.

Foreign purchases are covered in the foreign transactions article. Out-of-network ATM withdrawals on Everyday add a $2.50 Lumen fee on top of whatever the ATM operator charges.
