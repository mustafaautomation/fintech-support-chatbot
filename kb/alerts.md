# Alerts

Lumen can text or push when a purchase is over $100, when a foreign purchase posts, and when a payment is due in 3 days. Alerts are free in this demo.

This chat cannot change your phone number. If you are not getting alerts, a specialist checks the number on file. Lumen will not ask you to reply to an alert with a full card number or a one-time code. The sandbox code on the demo page is the only exception, and it is published because this is not a real bank.

Turn alerts on before you travel.
