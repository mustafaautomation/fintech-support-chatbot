# PIN and chip

The Everyday ATM PIN is chosen in the app. This chat cannot set or reveal a PIN. After three wrong ATM PIN attempts the ATM withdrawal declines until the next calendar day. Purchases with chip or wallet are not blocked by a PIN lock.

A chip error at a terminal is usually a retry, not fraud. If the plastic is damaged, report the card lost and we will send a replacement. The PIN does not move to the new card until you choose it again.

Never share a PIN in chat, by email, or with someone who calls you.
