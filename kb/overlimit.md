# Over-limit

Lumen Reserve declines a purchase that would put the balance over the credit limit. There is no over-limit fee in this demo. A payment that has not posted yet does not free the limit until it posts.

Pending authorizations, such as a hotel or a fuel pump, can hold an amount larger than the final charge. The hold usually drops in 3 days. The assistant can explain a hold. It cannot release a merchant hold.

If you need a higher limit, a specialist reviews the account. The chat will not promise an increase.
