# Direct deposit

Everyday can receive a paycheck by direct deposit. The fictional routing number for the demo is 021000021 and the account number is shown only in the app, never in this chat. We will not read it back.

Deposits before 8 p.m. Pacific on a business day are available the same night in this demo. A deposit does not raise the Reserve credit limit. Autopay can draw from the Everyday balance after a deposit posts.

If a deposit is missing, a specialist traces it. The assistant cannot see an employer payroll system.
