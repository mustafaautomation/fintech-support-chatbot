# Interest

Lumen Reserve purchases have a fictional variable APR of 19.99% when a balance revolves past the due date. If you pay the statement balance in full by the due date, new purchases in that cycle do not accrue purchase interest.

Cash advances accrue interest immediately. The cash-advance APR is 27.99% in this demo, separate from the purchase APR. Describing the rate is not a suggestion to carry a balance or take a cash advance.

Everyday is a debit card and does not have an APR. Interest is not charged on disputed amounts while a Reserve billing-error case is open.
